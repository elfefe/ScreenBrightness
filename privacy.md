# Privacy Policy — ScreenBrightness

**Effective date:** 31 July 2026
**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

ScreenBrightness is an Android application that dims the screen using a colour
overlay. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, and what it does
not.

---

## 1. What we do not collect

The app has **no account, no sign-in and no server of our own**. We never
collect, store or transmit:

* your name, email address, phone number or any contact details;
* your location, at any level of precision;
* your contacts, photos, files, messages or browsing history;
* the content of your screen. The overlay is drawn *on top of* the display —
  the app never reads or captures what is underneath.

We do not sell or rent data to anyone, because we do not hold any.

---

## 2. What stays on your device

Your settings — overlay darkness, chosen colour, schedule, and whether the
overlay is on — are stored **locally**, in Android's private preferences for
this app. They never leave the device, and Android deletes them when you
uninstall the app.

---

## 3. Crash reports

The app uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to know when it breaks. When a crash
occurs, it sends:

* the technical details of the crash (stack trace, and where in the code it
  happened);
* your device model, its Android version, and the app version;
* a random installation identifier, which is not linked to you and changes if
  you reinstall the app.

This is used only to find and fix bugs. See
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Firebase's data collection notice](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

---

## 4. Advertising

The app can display **one rewarded ad, and only when you ask for it** through
the *Watch an ad* menu entry. Nothing is ever shown on its own, and the app is
fully usable without ever watching one.

Before any ad is requested, we ask for your consent through Google's User
Messaging Platform. **If you decline, no advertising SDK is started at all** and
no advertising data leaves your device.

If you consent, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to
select and measure ads. Google is the data controller for that processing — see
[how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android
Settings, under *Privacy → Ads*.

---

## 5. Permissions the app asks for, and why

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Display over other apps | Draws the dimming overlay. Without it the app cannot work at all. |
| Notifications | Shows the ongoing notification used to dim, brighten or switch the overlay off. |
| Alarms & reminders | Turns the overlay on at the time you scheduled. Only requested when you set a schedule. |
| Run at startup | Restores your schedule after the device reboots. |
| Advertising ID | Only used if you consent to advertising. |

---

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any
personal information from children.

---

## 7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose,
correct or delete on request. For the data handled by Google as described in
sections 3 and 4, please refer to Google's own policies, or contact us and we
will help you get in touch with them.

You can withdraw your advertising consent at any time by clearing the app's
data in Android Settings; the consent form will be shown again on next launch.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the *Last updated* date above changes with it, and the
new version is published in the app's repository and store listing.
